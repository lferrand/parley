A conversation between two parties, the second still forming. Dark tile sits calmly in a Dock full of bright icons.
D · Sound into letter
Waveform on the left resolving into Hangul strokes on the right. The concept is the product. Busiest of the six; needs the most paring back.
E · Ribbon
Two threads passing each other — one language becoming another. No letters, no bubbles. Scales perfectly to 16 px, but the meaning is more felt than read.
F · Mic
Unmistakably "talk into this". Risk: it reads as a voice recorder, not a translator — nothing here says a second language exists.
